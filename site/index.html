YOUR ARCHIVE, ON YOUR DEVICE

Built with clear boundaries.

No Foliuna account, analytics or cloud library. Your chosen providers receive search and media requests; their own rules and privacy policies apply. Keys are encrypted locally and sent only to their configured API.

Windows uses an isolated Electron interface. Android uses bundled UI, Android Keystore credentials and private storage. Export excludes saved keys and private settings.

Safe-only is a search filter, not a guarantee of site moderation. Foliuna does not bypass provider restrictions or grant rights to redistribute artwork.
